Chapter 02

Give Every Peso a Purpose

Set a clear budget, then connect your spending plan to goals you actually care about.

Try the 50 / 30 / 20 framework

50% Needs: rent, groceries, transport, bills.

30% Wants: dining out, hobbies, entertainment.

20% Savings: emergency fund, debt reduction, future goals.

It's a starting point, not a universal rule. Adjust the proportions to your circumstances.

Chapter 03

Track It Before You Regret It

Little purchases add up. Track your spending without judgment, then look for patterns.

Where does your money really go?

Food delivery, coffee, subscriptions, quick online checkouts — hindi sila always bad. But awareness helps you decide whether they fit your priorities.

Try this: Record purchases for seven days. Review which ones were essential, enjoyable or impulsive.

Chapter 04

Protect Your Peace of Mind

Build a financial cushion and make space between the urge to buy and the decision to spend.

Emergency savings come first

An emergency fund can help cover unexpected expenses or an interruption in income. A common planning target is three to six months of essential expenses, depending on your situation.

Start small. Even regular deposits of ₱100 or ₱500 can build momentum.

Chapter 05

Make Room for Future Growth

Once your essentials and safety net are in good shape, learn how time, consistency and compounding can support longer-term goals.

Small contributions can add up

Investing carries risk, and returns are never guaranteed. But understanding compounding can help you plan for long-term goals with realistic expectations.

Remember: investment projections are illustrations, not promises. Consider risk, fees, inflation, and your personal circumstances.
